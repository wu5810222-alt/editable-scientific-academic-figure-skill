# Figure Review Checklist

Report findings in severity order.

## Scientific Logic

- Can the central contribution be stated after five seconds?
- Does every branch have a named output?
- Is the fusion/integration/update step shown?
- Are training and inference paths distinguished when both appear?
- Are claimed mechanisms supported by the paper rather than invented?

## Layout

- Is the main reading direction monotonic?
- Is canvas space used efficiently?
- Are visual weights balanced across stages?
- Are shared and modality-specific modules visually distinct?
- Do edges avoid unrelated nodes and text?

## Visual Language

- Does each color have one meaning?
- Are border widths, corner radii, and typography consistent?
- Are attention matrices, tokens, trajectories, or samples symbolic rather than noisy?
- Is the primary path stronger than auxiliary conditions?
- Does the figure remain readable in grayscale?

## Text And Formula

- Remove placeholders and raw code.
- Use short module names and explicit variables.
- Keep formulas beside their transformation.
- Check all line wrapping at the final export size.

## Editability

- Verify Draw.io contains native cells and connectors.
- Verify PPTX contains native shapes/connectors rather than one full-slide picture.
- Confirm real sample images are the only intended raster objects.

## Recommended Review Output

1. Critical scientific/narrative problems.
2. High-impact structural changes.
3. Visual refinements.
4. A proposed replacement wireframe.
5. Verification gaps or assumptions.
